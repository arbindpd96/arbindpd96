## What I do

I embed with enterprise teams, find the real problem, prototype fast, and carry the system through to production rollout. Then I codify the work: reusable connectors, custom SDKs, n8n automations and reference architectures, so the next engagement starts ahead.

At **AISquare Studio** I own the Explainability Layer and AWS infrastructure tracks across 5+ enterprise design partners in financial services and GRC. Before that I built production agentic AI single-handedly for a fintech platform, fine-tuned self-hosted LLMs back in 2020, and shipped an iOS app to a million downloads on my own.

## How I work

1. **Discover**: sit with risk and engineering stakeholders until the real problem is on the table.
2. **Scope**: turn ambiguous requirements into architecture documents, attestation formats and a demo plan people can say yes to.
3. **Prototype**: build the working demo fast.
4. **Ship**: production rollout with evaluation harnesses, guardrails, observability and zero-downtime deployment.
5. **Codify**: connectors, SDKs, n8n nodes, white papers and reference architectures, so the next client starts ahead.
